OVERVIEW

What this is

A data pipeline that joins marketing lead data with sales data, built on Databricks using PySpark and Spark SQL. It follows leads from first contact through to becoming a customer and generating revenue, and attributes that revenue back to the marketing channel that brought the lead in.

Built on the public Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset, using a medallion (bronze → silver → gold) architecture in Unity Catalog.

ARCHITECTURE

Architecture

Marketing and commerce data are processed as separate bronze/silver pipelines and join at the gold layer, where seller value is attributed back to the acquiring lead.

LayerMarketing funnelCommerce
Bronze Raw MQL + closed-deals CSVs, ingested as-is with _source_file / _ingested_at lineage columns 9 raw Olist CSVs (sellers, orders, items, payments, products, customers, reviews, geolocation, category translation)
Silver Deduplicated via window functions, categorical fields standardized, timeline anomalies flagged and quarantined — not dropped Orders/items/payments/products joined and rolled up to seller- and order-grain, with revenue reconciled to the cent
Gold Lead-level funnel, channel performance, landing-page performance, monthly trend Acquired-seller performance — post-acquisition orders/revenue only, attributed one record per seller via window-function dedup

Everything lands as managed Delta tables in Unity Catalog (workspace.default.*); raw files sit in a Unity Catalog volume.

DATA QUALITY

Data quality

Bad rows are flagged and quarantined, not deleted. One row has a negative sales-cycle-days value, flagged with has_invalid_sales_timeline and excluded from cycle-time averages. Each layer has its own quality checks — if revenue doesn't reconcile between layers within €0.01, the notebook fails instead of producing an incorrect result silently.

LIMITATIONS

Limitations

Runs on Databricks' free single-user workspace — no job scheduler, no multi-user access, no autoscaling. Notebooks run manually, in order; in production this would be a scheduled job or Delta Live Tables pipeline. The dataset is a fixed historical snapshot (2017–2018), not a live feed. There's no BI layer — results are queried from notebooks, not a dashboard.

WHAT'S NEXT

What's next

Schedule the pipeline as a proper job. Add a BI layer on the gold tables. Extend seller value to look at retention and churn, not just activation. Replace the inline asserts with unit tests.
